1'mote

2026

My current venture is creating 1'mote, a programmable infrared remote that can store many remotes' signals into one central device. 1'mote's goal is to replace cheap or bulky remotes with a rechargeable, practical, stylish, and all-in-one solution. It combines PCB design, firmware engineering, signal processing, hardware selection, and CAD modeling into one. Switch caps and case are currently being designed.
