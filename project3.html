Theremin Digital Twin

2025

The goal of this project was to design a theremin-like circuit and develop a chip layout in the Cadence software suite. In addition, the circuit was to be developed physically on a breadboard.The circuit needed a dual-oscillator setup, with the ability to control each oscillator’s frequency and output amplitude with a photoresistor, which effectively allows for theremin-like operation. The oscillators must operate roughly in the 20-20k Hz frequency range, as that is the range audible to human ears. Additional photoresistors controlled the amplitude of each oscillator's output. These photoresistors are to be arranged in a matrix as shown on the breadboard.My role in this project was to design the amplifier portion of the circuit and create the on-chip digital twin in Cadence.
